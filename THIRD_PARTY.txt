MotionWall downloads FFmpeg for the user's own local installation.
The source archive distributed here does not contain FFmpeg binaries.

Pinned provider: imageio-ffmpeg 0.6.0 platform wheels (arm64 and x86_64).
Project: https://github.com/imageio/imageio-ffmpeg
Release and published SHA-256 hashes: https://pypi.org/project/imageio-ffmpeg/0.6.0/
Binary provenance/build information: https://github.com/imageio/imageio-binaries
FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org/
FFmpeg source/releases: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
Licensing: https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

imageio-ffmpeg Python wrapper is BSD-2-Clause. Its bundled FFmpeg executable
has its own licensing, including GPL components in builds using libx264.
The exact binary configuration and license can be inspected with:
  ~/Applications/MotionWall.app/Contents/Resources/ffmpeg -version
  ~/Applications/MotionWall.app/Contents/Resources/ffmpeg -L

Do not redistribute an installed bundle containing FFmpeg without satisfying
the applicable FFmpeg/component source and license obligations. Keep this
source-and-downloader form for sharing the project.
